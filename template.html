The spot

Your westside living room

Tucked into El Paso's Upper Valley, Flip's is the neighborhood spot that does it all, a chill, friendly bar with a real kitchen, a serious wine list, and every game on the screens.

Food that's a step above the usual bar fare, ice-cold drinks, live music, and a patio happy hour worth clocking out early for. Bring the family, bring the dog, and settle in. Everybody's a regular here.
